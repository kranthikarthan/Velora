## Scoring and Interpretation

### Scoring Method
- Each category is worth 25 points
- Total possible score: 100 points
- Each sub-item is worth 1-2 points as indicated

### Readiness Levels

#### 90-100 Points: **Ready to Start**
- All critical requirements met
- Minimal risk factors
- Strong foundation for success
- **Recommendation**: Proceed with implementation

#### 75-89 Points: **Mostly Ready**
- Most requirements met
- Some minor gaps to address
- Low to moderate risk
- **Recommendation**: Address gaps and proceed

#### 60-74 Points: **Partially Ready**
- Significant gaps exist
- Moderate to high risk
- Additional preparation needed
- **Recommendation**: Address major gaps before starting

#### Below 60 Points: **Not Ready**
- Major gaps exist
- High risk of failure
- Significant preparation needed
- **Recommendation**: Complete preparation before starting

## Gap Analysis and Remediation

### Common Gaps and Solutions

#### Business Readiness Gaps
- **Lack of Executive Sponsorship**
  - Solution: Schedule executive briefings, demonstrate ROI
  - Timeline: 2-4 weeks

- **Unclear Requirements**
  - Solution: Conduct stakeholder workshops, document requirements
  - Timeline: 2-3 weeks

- **Budget Not Approved**
  - Solution: Create detailed business case, present to executives
  - Timeline: 1-2 weeks

#### Technical Readiness Gaps
- **Legacy System Access Issues**
  - Solution: Work with legacy system owners, get access agreements
  - Timeline: 2-4 weeks

- **Infrastructure Not Ready**
  - Solution: Provision infrastructure, configure environments
  - Timeline: 2-3 weeks

- **Security Concerns**
  - Solution: Conduct security review, address concerns
  - Timeline: 3-4 weeks

#### Team Readiness Gaps
- **Missing Key Team Members**
  - Solution: Accelerate hiring, consider contractors
  - Timeline: 4-6 weeks

- **Skills Gaps**
  - Solution: Provide training, hire specialists
  - Timeline: 2-4 weeks

- **Team Not Aligned**
  - Solution: Conduct team building, clarify roles
  - Timeline: 1-2 weeks

#### Operational Readiness Gaps
- **Support Processes Not Defined**
  - Solution: Define processes, train support team
  - Timeline: 2-3 weeks

- **Monitoring Not Configured**
  - Solution: Set up monitoring, configure alerts
  - Timeline: 1-2 weeks

- **Documentation Missing**
  - Solution: Create documentation, assign writers
  - Timeline: 2-3 weeks
